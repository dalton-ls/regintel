Skilled Nursing Facility Updates

The "Official" tab is the Skilled Nursing Facility (SNF) focused HHS/CMS Federal Register digest, plus GovInfo searches for "skilled nursing," "nursing homes," and "42 CFR 483," which contains the Conditions of Participation (CoPs) for SNFs. The "Context" tab includes industry reporting and secondary signals. SNF-relevant information will be directly tagged with "SNF Direct." The "Pages" tab includes links to CMS and related sources with no stable RSS. Excluded: care-setting agnostic regulatory updates (e.g., OSHA), other CoPs or Conditions for Coverage (CfCs) that only apply to some SNFs (e.g., hospice CfCs). This is a notification layer; product change implications are not surfaced. Weeks run Sunday-Saturday.
